A few reminders

Keep this close.

When should I ask for professional help? +

Consider reaching out when distressing changes last around two weeks or more, affect sleep, appetite, concentration, enjoyment, or your ability to complete usual tasks. You can also ask earlier if something feels difficult to carry alone.

How can I support a friend? +

Listen without rushing to fix, ask what kind of support they want, encourage professional help when needed, and take immediate safety concerns seriously. You can care deeply without becoming their only support.

Is this website a diagnosis or counselling service? +

No. This is an educational student project and a starting point for reflection. It cannot diagnose, treat, or replace a qualified mental health professional.
